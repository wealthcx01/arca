# ARCA-62 — Brand & Visual Identity Redesign: Premium Terminal, Earned Not Decorative

**Status:** Todo · **Area:** Design/Brand · **Depends on:** —

## Why this matters (for the founder)
ARCA currently looks like a developer tool, not something collectors trust with real money. A redesigned identity — grounded in ARCA's real data edge (the ARCA Score, grading alpha, market coverage no one else has) — should make the product feel like a premium financial terminal that has earned its polish, not just applied it.

## Context
- Founder decision: the brand should communicate "the numbers here are real." Premium because it's earned through proprietary data (ARCA Score, grading alpha, unique market coverage), not decorative styling. Saved to venture knowledge as `arca-brand-positioning`.
- Current baseline: ARCA's UI has been modeled loosely on trading-terminal references (e.g. TRKD Market Monitor) but reads as dev-tool-like rather than art-directed.
- Market signal: premium fintech/trading-terminal design in 2025-26 trends toward high-contrast, restrained, dark or black-and-gold-leaning palettes and bold minimal typography, deliberately away from generic corporate blue and decorative flourishes — consistent with a "trust with real money" feel.
- This is a brand/visual-identity change, not a features or data change.

## Scope
- New logo
- New colour palette (high-contrast, restrained, premium-finance tone — not generic corporate blue)
- New typography system
- Component/UI restyle applying the new identity across existing app screens (dashboard, screener, watchlist, etc.)
- Design rationale ties visibly back to ARCA's data credibility (ARCA Score, grading alpha, market coverage) rather than pure decoration

## Out of scope
- No changes to features, data, scoring logic, or how the app functions
- No new pages/surfaces beyond restyling what exists today
- No pricing or positioning copy changes (handled separately if needed)

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] New logo delivered in usable formats
- [ ] Documented colour palette and typography system
- [ ] All existing app screens restyled to the new system with no functional regressions
- [ ] Design rationale explicitly connects the "premium" feel to ARCA's data (Score, grading alpha, coverage), not just visual polish
