# Market note: the terminal wedge and beachhead

## ARCA market note

Serious graded-card investors want a **terminal**, not a price lookup.

### Competitor pricing
- Card Ladder: $150/year — institutional analytics.
- Market Movers: $10/month — hobbyist scanning.

### The wedge
Graded cards where population data already exists, and nobody has built **screening** on top of it.

### Beachhead
Active flippers who trade for margin — not collectors tracking a personal collection's value.
